Delete your account

Into the Pond is a parent-operated app. You can delete your account from the app (Sanctuary Gate → Delete account) or request deletion by email below.

Your profile and journal entries are removed immediately. You have 30 days to sign back in and cancel deletion. After that, deletion is permanent.

Deleting your account does not cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Manage billing in your device settings.

Already signed in? Open the Into the Pond app → Sanctuary Gate → scroll to Delete account.
